# Release notes

Highlights per version, newest first, with 1.x condensed. 2.5 is the version distributed; the entries below it show how the app got there.

## 2.5 - November 2026

- **Top cards that scroll away:** in Browse and the Queue, the top card scrolls away with the list, as the opening screen's cards do, where it stayed in place.
- **Room settings from the room:** a tap on the room's name in the mini player, or in the row at Now Playing's foot, opens Room settings, as the icon on the Rooms card does, with that room open. TalkBack offers it as one of the mini player's actions.
- **The heart in the mini player:** from 600dp wide, as on a tablet either way round, the mini player has Now Playing's heart too, left of Play/Pause and as large. A tap saves or removes what plays as a favourite, and the opening screen's Favorites card and Favorites in Browse follow at once. It reads and saves them at the same speaker as that card, whichever room plays: each speaker keeps its own copy of the favourites, which follows a change only a while later. A narrower window in split screen leaves it out, as a phone does.
- **Green hearts:** the heart fills in green while what plays is a favourite, as good news, where it was blue.
- **Servers on the WiFi only:** a server must be on the phone's own WiFi, as a room's speaker must; a remembered address elsewhere is no longer used. An address in brackets must be a real IPv6 address.
- **Sturdier:** **Scan for new content** is followed for half an hour at most, where a speaker that never said it was done kept the icon blue. A cover being fetched as a screen is left is no longer fetched twice, and a wordy server's answer to the search fits. A track length out of range, such as more hours than the app can count, counts as none, where it could show as negative. A reply with a malformed length or chunk is refused. A room whose play mode or queue can't be read no longer shows as unreachable: the rest of what it answered shows. A play that fails puts shuffle back on. The Favorites card counts every favourite, not only the first 100. **Not supported on this source.** ends with a full stop, as the other messages do.
- **Lighter:** each reply is checked and read in one pass, with one XML parser maker for all of them; Browse works out its count line and **▶** as the pages come, rather than going through the whole level each time; a group's volume reuses its threads; and Room settings reads a room's settings all at once.

## 2.4 - November 2026

- **Progress in the mini player:** the edge of the Play/Pause circle fills clockwise from the top as the track plays, white in dark mode and grey in light mode. A radio stream or the TV, which have no length, show none. Switching to a station clears it at once, and Now Playing's bar greys out at once, where both kept the track's progress until the station played.
- **The mini player stays:** it shows whenever there's a room to control, also with nothing loaded, as **No content**. It waited for the room's first item and then stayed for the rest of the session, so whether it showed depended on what had played before.
- **Controls together on a tablet:** Now Playing's controls stand at most 32dp apart, in the middle, where on a wide screen they spread to its edges; the progress bar and the volume keep the full width. A phone's stay as they were.
- **Previous at once:** the time goes to 0:00 as soon as Previous is tapped, as for a seek; it could wait for the next poll, up to the Polling interval later, while the speaker still reported the old place.
- **No flicker:** Play/Pause and mute no longer flip back for a moment when a poll already under way brings the old state, and the Queue's playing track no longer jumps back after a tap or a **✕**. Several **✕** in a row no longer bring a row back for a moment: the list is read again once the last of them is done.
- **Sturdier:** **Clear queue** takes its turn as it's confirmed: something played since, also from another screen, wins, where a clear that came later could empty it. Before a tap or a **✕** in the Queue, the track at that place is checked by its link as well as its title, artist and album. The Queue no longer keeps the hidden mini player's clock ticking. TalkBack names Now Playing's progress bar **Position**.

## 2.3 - November 2026

- **Back from the Queue:** a **‹** at its top left goes back to Now Playing, as Back does. The Queue no longer shows the mini player, so its list runs to the foot of the screen; the volume buttons still set the room's volume there. **Clear queue** says the music will stop only while the room plays its queue: a favourite played on its own, a radio station or the TV plays on.
- **The mini player apart:** a stronger grey edge sets it apart from the cards above it, also when one scrolls right up to it. A title too long for it slides back as fast as it went, where it went back twice as fast.
- **A blue heart:** while what plays is a favourite, the heart on Now Playing is filled in blue, as Shuffle and Repeat light up, where it was in the text's colour.
- **Covers of favourites:** a Music library track saved as a favourite on another speaker shows its cover too.
- **Sturdier:** an album's **Play now** or **Replace queue** takes its turn as it's chosen: something played while its tracks load, as a favourite, now wins, where the album could still empty the queue after it; an album overtaken that way also stops loading its tracks. A speaker's error that comes with a prefix, as some send it, is read as such, so a queue that changed meanwhile or a favourite saved already is still handled as one. A reply in a service's namespace with a version that isn't a number no longer counts as an answer.

## 2.2 - November 2026

- **Favorites from Now Playing:** the heart saves what plays as a Sonos favourite, filled while it is one, in the text's colour as a room's ✓; a tap on the filled heart removes it. For a track from a server or the Music library, and for a music service's radio station, which then show in the Sonos app as well, as if saved there; a server's track shows in UPnP Cast's Favorites only, as the Sonos app hides those. A favourite saved or removed in the Sonos app shows here too, read again as the track changes and as Now Playing opens; Favorites reads its list again each time it shows, as after Now Playing.
- **Remove a favourite:** in Favorites as a list, a tap on the **✕** at a favourite's end removes it at once, as in the Queue. A server track's favourite now shows its cover.
- **The room's row on Now Playing:** at its foot, as in the Sonos app, the queue icon at the left, where the Queue button was, the room in the middle, no longer in the line under the title, and the heart at the right.
- **Music sooner:** a tap on a track in a long list, **▶**, **Play now** or **Replace queue** plays as soon as that track is in the queue, the rest going in while it plays; the speaker takes 16 tracks per call, so 500 tracks took about two seconds before the music started. Another tap meanwhile, or **Clear queue**, stops the rest from going in and follows at once, where it waited for all of them; **Play next** and **Add to end of queue** wait their turn. Changes to a room's queue take turns, also from different screens. With shuffle on, it goes back on once all are in. Should the rest fail to go in, the music plays on and the app says **Unable to add to queue.**
- **The Queue at once:** its first hundred tracks show as soon as the speaker sends them, about a tenth of a second, the rest following while the list is in use, as in Browse; it waited for the whole queue, half a second for 500 tracks. Read again, as after a **✕**, the list stays as it is until the whole queue is in.
- **Albums apart:** an album remembers list or tiles by itself, so its numbered tracks can stay a list while categories, folders and Favorites show as tiles, or the other way round. A first install starts that way: albums as a list, the rest as tiles.
- **Sturdier:** the Queue reads all its pages from one revision of the queue: a track added or removed meanwhile, as by another controller or a fill still going in, no longer leaves a removed track in the list or skips another; the list is read again, a few times if it keeps changing. A favourite keeps its format and cover however the speaker spells its metadata.
- **Version in App settings:** the version and copyright line is the last line of App settings, where it no longer hides behind the mini player.
- **Even spacing on Now Playing:** the rows under the cover stand 20dp apart, as from the controls down, and the cover takes the room that frees; the mini player's two rows stand as far apart.

## 2.1 - November 2026

- **Group rooms on the Rooms card:** every room has its circle, as in the Sonos app: ✓ for the rooms that play together, ○ for the others. A tap on a room chooses the one the player controls, its name in blue; a tap on its circle groups or ungroups it. The **Group rooms** setting is gone. The ✓ is in the text's colour, so blue marks the chosen room alone.
- **Previous as in most players:** once a track has played more than 5 seconds, Previous goes back to its start; within 5 seconds, to the track before; on the first track, to its start again. On a radio stream or the TV it stays the speaker's own. Next on the last track does nothing, where it said the room couldn't be reached.
- **Clearer messages:** a room that answers but refuses, as Pause on the TV, says **Not supported on this source**; **Couldn't connect** is kept for a room that doesn't answer at all.
- **Touch feedback that fits:** every icon button lights up in the same 40dp circle, whole, also where its row or card cut it off before, as a room's circle and a tile's **⋯**. Previous and Next fill their grey circle, as Play does. Rows, tiles and choices light up within their rounded corners, and the rows and tiles in Browse, Favorites and the Queue now light up at all.
- **Bigger targets:** App settings and Favorites open with a tap anywhere on their card, as a room's row does; a room's circle takes a tap 48dp wide.
- **Upright on a phone:** the screens no longer turn on a phone; on a tablet, from 600dp wide, they turn as before.
- **More room between settings:** in App settings and Room settings, each setting stands as far from the next, switches included.
- **The playing track in blue:** the Queue marks it by its colour alone, without the bars before it; TalkBack reads it as **Current track**.
- **Remove a track from the queue:** a tap on the **✕** at a track's end removes it at once, as the Sonos app's swipe does. The track at that place is checked first, and the speaker removes it only if the queue hasn't changed since, as from another controller; otherwise the list is read again.
- **Sample rates in kHz:** a track Sonos can't play says so as **Can't play Festival. It's encoded at 96 kHz; Sonos plays up to 48 kHz.**, where it gave the rate in Hz. A rate between whole kHz shows exactly, as `22.05 kHz`, where it was rounded to `22.1 kHz`.
- **Sturdier:** a room's reply must be the answer to what was asked, one whole SOAP document: a web page, an empty or unfinished reply, another action's answer, or that answer's tag in a comment no longer counts as done, so what depends on it, such as Play after a new queue, isn't sent. A station's name that couldn't be read is asked again after a minute rather than at every poll, so it can't hold up the controls. A server shows as soon as it answers, its icon following, where a slow icon could hold it back up to 15 seconds.

## 2.0 - November 2026

- **Long titles slide:** in the mini player, a title too long for its row slides to its end and back once, as it changes and as the screen shows again, its edges fading while it moves, then ends in "…" again. Not with Android's animations off, as under **Remove animations**.
- **Sturdier:** a track whose title is its file name, as **Episode.mp3** on a share without tags, shows again: since 1.5 it showed nothing, not even its format or cover. A favourite playlist or album starts at its first track even when another controller queues something meanwhile. A change in Room settings no longer jumps back to the old value when a read that was already under way lands after it. A failed read of the speakers' list of music services waits a minute before it's tried again, so it can't hold up Play/Pause. Browsing one server while a track from another plays no longer leaves that track without its cover in the mini player and Now Playing. With shuffle on, a track tapped, or chosen with **Play now**, plays itself, not another of the queue's; shuffle stays on for the rest.
- **Track numbers in albums:** an album shows each track's number before its title, in grey, such as **3. Festival**, in its list and in its tiles: the number the server has from the file's tags, so an album with tracks missing keeps its own numbering. An album on several discs adds the disc, as **2-3.**. Other lists show titles alone.

## 1.x - November 2026

The first release, then the Music library, group play, Room settings and Favorites.

- **1.0** - A UPnP/DLNA music library on Sonos, browsed and played from Android. It finds the rooms and servers by itself and stays on the WiFi; Browse shows a server's own categories as a list or as tiles, with covers and each track's format; a tap plays a track with the rest of the list queued around it, and **⋯** offers Play now, Play next, Add to end of queue and Replace queue. The speaker fetches the music itself. A mini player, Now Playing and the Queue, in the Sonos app's terms; files up to 48 kHz, Sonos's limit over UPnP; Appearance and a Polling interval. 153 KB, no libraries, nothing running in the background.
- **1.1** - Android 14 and 15 as well; **Home** in Browse; Spotify on the speaker with its cover and whether it's lossless; a source without track details named, as TV, and "Not supported on this source" for a control it doesn't offer; Now Playing's progress counting evenly; the mini player from the room's first item; a new icon.
- **1.2** - The Sonos **Music library**, with **Scan for new content**; **▶** in Browse to play a level's tracks; the phone's volume buttons, by the new **Volume button step**; the TV's input format; settings that fold away; and sturdier requests: on the WiFi and to addresses only, with no endless paging, and sliders that follow a keyboard and TalkBack.
- **1.3** - **Group rooms**, with one volume for the rooms together; every screen following grouping done elsewhere, as in the Sonos app; Now Playing at once, its cover larger on taller screens; the source on Now Playing, such as `MinimServer · FLAC 96 kHz 24-bit`; room icons by speaker kind, with the model; new colours.
- **1.4** - **Room settings**, each room's own as in the Sonos app: Trueplay, EQ, Volume limit, Sub audio, Home theater and Hardware; **Quick skip**; Previous and Next in circles; colour that means something, green for good news and orange for problems; a progress bar that keeps moving; caps on a cover's size and a list's length.
- **1.5** - **Favorites**, played in the chosen room; radio as the Sonos app shows it, with the station's name and logo; music services by name, such as **Qobuz**; even margins and calmer corners; Room settings sliding open; and the largest of a server's covers.
